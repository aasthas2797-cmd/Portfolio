Areas of Focus.

Three fields, three different questions — hover or tap to explore my perspective.

Climate Strategy & Risk

Exploring how climate risks shape business decisions — what the evidence shows, what remains uncertain, and which trade-offs matter.

My Perspective

Two businesses can face the same climate risk and arrive at different decisions. That raises questions worth exploring: What are they measuring? Which assumptions are they relying on? How do cost, timing and uncertainty influence their choices?

For me, the conclusion is only as useful as the reasoning behind it — and whether the proposed response can actually work.

Circular Economy

How far can a resource go before it becomes waste?Exploring how design, reuse and business decisions can extend the life of materials and preserve their value.

My Perspective

I've always paid attention to waste that feels unnecessary — materials discarded too soon, energy used without much thought, or resources consumed without a clear reason.

But recognising waste is the easy part. The more useful questions are why it happens, whether it could have been avoided, and what a better alternative would require. Sometimes the answer lies in design, behaviour, or the economics of how a business operates.

Business Analysis & Operations

A waste figure, a supplier quote or a missed deadline rarely tells the whole story. Understanding what’s driving the result, weighing the options and deciding what to do next.

My Perspective

Working in operations has taught me that even a sensible plan can fall apart when priorities compete, resources are limited, or circumstances change.

It has also shaped how I approach a problem. Before recommending a solution, I want to understand what's causing it, question the assumptions and compare the alternatives. Then comes the practical test: Who needs to make it work? What could get in the way? And how will we know if it succeeded?

BACKGROUND & PROFESSIONAL DRIVE

A practical approach to complex questions.

Different experiences. A broader perspective. A deliberate direction.

There is often a considerable distance between a company's climate ambitions and what its operations can realistically deliver.

The more I explore climate risk and the circular economy, the clearer one challenge becomes: understanding the frameworks is only part of the work. The harder questions concern the evidence behind decisions, the commercial constraints involved, and the systems needed to make those decisions effective. A reporting framework alone doesn't reduce waste or manage risk. What happens beyond it matters.

I wanted to go further than studying regulations and their requirements. My independent projects give me the opportunity to examine how those requirements translate into real business questions — testing assumptions, analysing evidence, evaluating trade-offs and developing recommendations that can be explained and defended.

I'm continuing to build that depth of expertise, with the ambition to take on more complex challenges and contribute not only to the decisions businesses make, but to how those decisions are delivered.
