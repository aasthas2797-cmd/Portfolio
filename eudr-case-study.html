01 / THE ASSESSMENT AT A GLANCE

When documentation looks complete, what is still missing?

For fictional importer Meridian Foods Europe B.V., an Excel decision model maps three Indonesian refined-palm-oil sourcing routes to twelve production plots, evidence records, refinery batches and planned shipments.

The key question is not simply whether a document exists. It is whether the evidence is relevant, usable and linked to the exact material a business intends to source.

01 CONTEXT

Business challenge & scope

A commercially useful purchasing record is not automatically sufficient to demonstrate where a product originated.

3 routes · 12 plots · 3 shipments
02 REGULATION

What EUDR requires

Article 9 origin data, Article 10 substantive risk assessment and Article 11 mitigation are separate steps. A documentation pass is not legal clearance.

Requirements → decision rules
03 METHOD

Working backwards from a shipment

Batch identifiers connect collector, mill, refinery and contributing plots. Missing evidence follows those links into downstream decisions.

04 EVIDENCE

Traceability & record review

Five evidence records per plot expose different gaps: origin geometry, custody, legality, production period and land-use indicators.

05 EXPOSURE

Findings that change the decision

Five unresolved plot exceptions affect 46 t under segregation. Under a fully blended assumption, the same issues affect all 70 t.

06 TRADE-OFFS

Options & financial sensitivity

Conditional combined storage and replacement-supply scenarios range from €1,656 to €12,420 for the 46 t segregated case—not forecasts of actual loss.

07 RECOMMENDATION

What should be done next?

Retain an internal hold on affected shipments; seek evidence-specific remediation and reassess substantive risk before any placement conclusion.

08 GOVERNANCE

Controls & implementation

Assign corrective action to procurement and compliance reviewers; close an issue only once the source evidence, linkage and decision have been reassessed.

Evidence owner · reviewer · decision trail
09 CONCLUSION

What the assessment establishes

Strong record availability can conceal concentrated sourcing exposure. The model clarifies what can be tested—and what still needs independent verification.

Conclusions · limitations · references
04 / THE BUSINESS DECISION

The important result isn't the percentage. It's the decision it changes.

For SHP-02, adding a missing geolocation record does not release the 28-tonne shipment: the custody link remains unresolved. Even when both documentary exceptions are corrected, the batch advances to Pending Article 10, not regulatory clearance.

That's the distinction this case study is designed to make visible: records, relationships, uncertainty and commercial consequences must be assessed together.
